How This Works

This system calculates the exact stake for every trade so that — if you land your target number of wins anywhere within the cycle — your capital grows to the projected final value, no matter the order wins and losses land in. Every box on the left is editable and the entire trade table, every stat, and every stake amount recalculates live as you change inputs or log results.

Your Capital — the total amount you start this cycle with.
Total Trades — how many trades make up one full cycle.
Wins Needed — how many of those trades must win for the cycle to close successfully.
Payout / Quota — your broker's payout multiplier (e.g. 1.93 on Quotex = 93% profit per win).
